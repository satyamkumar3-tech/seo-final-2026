## Current official facts

- CBIC lists articles of jewellery under HSN 7113 at 1.5% central tax plus 1.5% state or union territory tax, or 3% integrated tax.
- CBIC's gems and jewellery sectoral FAQ says 3% applies to the total transaction value of jewellery whether making charges are shown separately or not.
- The same sectoral FAQ says 5% applies to a registered job worker's job charges. This is an upstream business treatment, not an extra retail levy to add to the customer's jewellery transaction value.
- CBIC's general FAQ says that when old gold is exchanged while buying a new 20 gram set, GST is charged on the open market value of the entire 20 gram set under the stated valuation rule.
- BIS says a hallmarked jewellery invoice should state the article description, net precious-metal weight, purity in carat and fineness, and hallmarking charges. It also tells consumers they can verify purity through a BIS recognized Assaying and Hallmarking centre.
