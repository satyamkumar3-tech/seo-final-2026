History of Republic Day

History connects Independence Day to the day India chose a written Constitution as its supreme law.

On 26 January 1930, the Indian National Congress declared Purna Swaraj, complete self rule, as the national goal.

Independence arrived on 15 August 1947, yet the country still needed a full constitutional framework.

The Constituent Assembly drafted the Constitution under Dr. B. R. Ambedkar's chairmanship of the Drafting Committee.

Keep these quick takeaways handy:

  1. The Constitution was adopted on 26 November 1949 and came into force on 26 January 1950.
  2. Choosing 26 January linked the new Republic to the earlier freedom declaration and its unfinished promise.
  3. Dr. Rajendra Prasad became the first President of the Republic of India.
  4. Since then, 26 January has been observed as Republic Day with growing national ceremony and public participation.
  5. History reminds us that freedom needed a rulebook; Republic Day honours that rulebook coming alive.
