## 2. Regulatory & Quality Standards (BIS Hallmarking)
- **Authority:** Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS), Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution, Government of India.
- **Mandatory 3 Marks on Gold Jewellery:**
  1. **BIS Triangular Standard Logo**: The official hallmark emblem certifying purity.
  2. **Purity and Fineness Mark**: Explicitly stamped on the inner shank:
     - `22K916` (91.6% pure gold, traditional yellow gold)
     - `18K750` (75.0% pure gold, ideal balance of durability, luster, and stone setting security)
     - `14K585` (58.5% pure gold, highest tensile strength and scratch resistance for active daily wear)
  3. **Hallmark Unique Identification (HUID)**: A unique 6-digit alphanumeric code laser-etched on every individual piece, verifiable by consumers via the official BIS Care App.
- **Invoice Standards:** Retail invoices must explicitly state gross weight, net gold weight (excluding stones, enamel, or pearls), stone weights in carats, making charges, and hallmarking charges.

## 3. Statutory Taxation
- **Goods and Services Tax (GST):** Uniform 3% statutory GST applied on the combined value of gold metal and making charges across India.

## 4. Indian Ring Sizing Standards for Girls & Adolescents
- Standard Indian ring sizing system corresponds to inner circumference and diameter in millimeters:
  - **Size 6:** Inner Diameter ~14.6 mm | Inner Circumference ~45.9 mm (petite pre-teens / small fingers)
  - **Size 8:** Inner Diameter ~15.3 mm | Inner Circumference ~48.0 mm
  - **Size 10:** Inner Diameter ~15.9 mm | Inner Circumference ~50.0 mm (most common teenage girl size)
  - **Size 12:** Inner Diameter ~16.5 mm | Inner Circumference ~51.8 mm
  - **Size 14:** Inner Diameter ~17.2 mm | Inner Circumference ~54.0 mm
- **Measurement Best Practices:** Measure finger at room temperature at the end of the day when fingers are at their natural resting size; ensure ring slips smoothly over knuckles with slight resistance; opt for comfort-fit bands with softly domed inner shanks.

## 5. Structural Engineering & Setting Architecture for Girls
- **Low-Profile Settings:** Bezel, semi-bezel, and flush (gypsy) settings protect gemstones against active daily impact, sports, and school activities without snagging delicate fabrics or knitwear.
- **Prong Architecture:** If prongs are used (e.g., solitaires, floral clusters), four-prong rounded or claw-tipped prongs set low into the gallery prevent catching.
- **Comfort Fit vs Standard Fit:** Slightly domed interior surface of the band reduces friction and skin moisture entrapment, preventing skin irritation.
- **Hypoallergenic Alloys:** High-grade nickel-free 14K and 18K yellow, rose, and white gold alloys ensure no contact dermatitis for sensitive teenage skin.

## 6. Symbolism & Cultural Finger Placement
- **Ring Finger (Fourth Finger):** Associated with the sun and emotional devotion; traditionally associated with rings of affection, promise, and celebratory gifting.
- **Index Finger:** Represents ambition, leadership, and personal milestone celebration (often chosen by young girls styling their own daily signets or cocktail rings).
- **Middle Finger:** Symbolizes balance, focus, and independence; popular for geometric, minimalist, and statement motifs.
- **Pinky Finger:** Represents communication, intuition, and individuality (often styled with dainty bands or petite signet rings).
