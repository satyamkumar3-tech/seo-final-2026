## Verified measurement methods

- For a closed slip-on bangle without an existing reference, bring the fingers together as if putting on a bangle. Wrap a flexible tape or thin paper strip around the widest part of the hand and knuckles. Measure that hand circumference, then match it to the chart.
- If a comfortable bangle is available, place it flat and measure straight across the centre between the inner edges. Use the inner diameter only. Do not include the metal thickness or outer edges.
- A closed bangle must pass over the widest part of the hand, so wrist circumference alone is not the correct measurement for a rigid closed bangle.
- For a hinged, openable, or adjustable design, follow the individual product sizing instructions because the opening mechanism can change which measurement matters.
- If a measurement sits between listed closed-bangle sizes, the safer home-selection rule is to choose the next larger size, then confirm against the individual product guide.

## Editorial cautions

- Treat the chart as a conversion guide, not a universal fit guarantee. Product shape, width, hinge construction, and seller-specific tolerances can affect fit.
- Do not state a universal most-common size or claim that a particular size fits every adult.
- Do not claim every metal bangle can or cannot be resized. Resizing depends on design, construction, stones, and the jeweller's assessment.
- Do not present wrist size and closed-bangle size as interchangeable.
- Do not use prices, exchange guarantees, or return-policy claims without checking the specific live product policy.
